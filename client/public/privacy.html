Privacy

Last updated 8 August 2026

nightraze does not ask who you are. There is no account, no email, no password and no payment. You type a name, you play, and the game keeps the little it needs to run an arena, stop cheating, and know whether anyone is out there.

Who is responsible

nightraze.io is operated by Impossible Labs Ltd., a company based in Gibraltar, which is the data controller for everything described below. You can reach us at info_AT_impossiblelabs_DOT_xyz — written that way to keep the address out of the hands of scrapers; replace _AT_ with @ and _DOT_ with a dot.

What stays in your browser

The game stores a few values on your own device. These are not cookies and are not sent anywhere except where noted below.

  • The name you last played under, so you do not retype it.
  • Your personal best light, so the start screen can show what you are playing against.
  • Your touch control preference, and whether you have seen the tutorial.
  • A random identifier (nightraze.visitor.v1) generated on your device. It is a random value, not derived from you or your hardware, and exists so we can count returning players without knowing who they are.

Clearing your browser's site data for nightraze.io erases all of it, including the random identifier. A new one is generated the next time you play, and the old one cannot be reconnected to it.

What the server records

  • A hashed identifier. The random identifier above is sent when you connect, and the server stores only a SHA-256 hash of it. The raw value is never written to disk.
  • A coarse location. Either a two-letter country code from our hosting provider, or your browser's time zone (for example Europe/Rome). Never a precise location.
  • Session and connection quality. When a session started and ended, and round-trip latency samples, so we can tell whether the arena is playable in your region.
  • Crash events. The hashed identifier plus a short category such as window-error. No stack traces, no page contents, no personal data.

These records are deleted after 90 days.

IP addresses and anti-cheat

Your IP address is used while you are connected to limit how many simultaneous connections one source may open. It is not logged as analytics and is not stored for ordinary play.

It is stored in one case only: if the server's anti-cheat suspends a connection, the IP is written down with the suspension so a repeat offence lengthens the next one. Suspensions last up to seven days, and the record is deleted 30 days after the most recent one.

Your player name is public

The name you choose is shown to everyone in the arena and on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or anything you would not put in front of strangers. Names are filtered for profanity and fall back to a default when rejected.

Playing on CrazyGames and other portals

nightraze is listed on game portals, which embed the game in their own page. When you play through one:

  • The portal's own privacy policy governs everything the portal does, including any advertising and any account you hold with them. That is between you and them.
  • If you are signed in to the portal, the game asks it for your username and avatar so your friends can recognise you on the leaderboard. We do not receive your email address or password, and we do not store the username on our servers beyond the ordinary session records above.
  • The values listed under "what stays in your browser" are kept through the portal's storage rather than ours, which is what lets your name and best score follow you across their regional sites.

What we do not do

  • No cookies.
  • No third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No advertising network of our own, and no data sold or shared for advertising.
  • No profiling, and no attempt to identify you across sites.

Children

nightraze is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because there are no accounts, there is nothing for a child to sign up for.

Your choices and your rights

Clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device and severs any link to the hashed records on the server, which then expire on the schedule above. That is the fastest and most complete option available to you, and it needs no one's permission.

Under Gibraltar data protection law, and under the GDPR where it applies to you, you have rights to access, correct, erase, and restrict the processing of personal data about you, and to object to it. You can exercise them by writing to the address above.

One honest limitation. We store a hash of a random identifier and never the identifier itself, so given only your name or email we cannot find the rows that belong to you — there is nothing in them that points back to a person. That is a consequence of collecting as little as we do, not an evasion, and it is why clearing site data is the more effective route. If you still want us to try, we will need the raw identifier from your browser's storage, and we will explain how to find it.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first — but you are entitled to complain to the data protection supervisory authority in Gibraltar, or to the one where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify, by design.
